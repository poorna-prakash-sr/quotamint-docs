# Customers and credits

QuotaMint keeps the customer identifier that your application already uses.
You do not need to migrate customer authentication or payment data.

In the current preview, create customers and assign plans from the dashboard.
Your application can then use the public runtime API to record usage and enforce feature access.

## Customer lifecycle

A customer can be:

- **Active**: usage requests can be allowed when the customer has an eligible plan and balance.
- **Inactive**: the balance and history remain available, but new usage requests are denied.
- **Archived**: the customer is removed from normal active lists while its historical records remain available according to your retention period.

Customer IDs cannot be changed after creation.
If your application changes an identifier, create or synchronize a new customer record instead of silently reusing the old one.

## Credit balances

Credits can come from:

- Monthly plan allocations
- Manual grants or top-ups managed by your team
- Credit adjustments or refunds
- Charges created by successful `consume` requests

A successful credit consumption returns `creditsUsed` and `creditsRemaining`.
An insufficient balance returns a denied decision; it does not create a partial charge.

## Monthly allocations

Monthly included credits are applied once per active customer each calendar month.
The account plan's monthly event limit is separate from the credits included in your product plans.

Your account plan controls how much QuotaMint activity your workspace can record.
Your product plan controls how many credits a customer receives.

## Usage history

The dashboard shows usage events, feature or event name, quantity, customer, environment, and timestamps.
Use this history to answer:

- Which customer used a feature?
- Which feature consumed credits?
- How much usage did a customer generate?
- When did a balance reach zero?

Retention depends on your workspace account plan.
See [Limits and plans](/limits) for the current retention windows.

## What QuotaMint does not store

QuotaMint uses customer identifiers and optional display data for metering.
It does not need your customers' passwords, card numbers, or payment credentials.
Keep sensitive application data out of metadata unless you need it for usage context and your data policy allows it.

# QuotaMint documentation

QuotaMint helps you control what each customer can use in your application.
You define plans and features in the dashboard, then call QuotaMint from your backend when a customer uses a metered action.

QuotaMint covers:

- Feature access and plan entitlements
- Credit balances and per-use charges
- Usage-only metrics such as tokens, minutes, or documents
- Customer plan assignment
- Test and Live environments
- Usage history, alerts, and signed webhooks

QuotaMint does **not** process payments or act as an API gateway.
Keep your existing billing provider and call QuotaMint after your application has decided that a customer or organization changed plans.

<Note>
  QuotaMint is currently a developer-preview product. Features, availability, and plan limits may change before production launch.
</Note>

## How the integration works

1. You create a workspace and project in the dashboard.
2. You create plans and features, including the credit cost for each credit-based feature.
3. You create customers using the identifiers already used by your application.
4. Your backend calls `consume` when a customer performs a chargeable action.
5. Your application uses the response to allow the action, show an upgrade message, or retry safely.

Your backend remains responsible for authentication, payments, and the application action itself.
QuotaMint supplies the usage decision and records the result.
